IMERS is a fictional branding project for MSK Ghent museum that introduces young creatives to classical art in a contemporary way. The goal is to create a creative, immersive brand inspired by the museum's medieval paintings, brought to life through posters, animations and web design.

Para Ti is a small automated neighbourhood store where you can buy fresh, local food. To give their brand a glow up, I created a completely new visual identity, from logo, colour palette, business card and typography to social media assets, that still reflects the warm community atmosphere around their shops.
